Bio

Hailing from Manchester, Mothbreather blend heavy art-rock and psychedelic post-punk. Jumping seamlessly between massive riffs, delicate chords and jumpy time signatures, Mothbreather are built for chaotic rooms, unpredictable bills, and fans of the weird.

Mothbreather are:

  • Justin Pritchard - Drums
  • Tom Hammersley - Bass and Vocals
  • Dean Lavery - Guitar
